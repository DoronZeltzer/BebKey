# Olim chadashim - real estate benefits and tax breaks

If you've made aliyah within the past 7 years, Israeli law gives you a meaningful set of benefits for buying or renting your first Israeli home. They're not advertised loudly - the burden is on you to know what to claim and when. Most olim leave money on the table because nobody told them.

Here's the thorough list as of 2026, with the eligibility windows and how to apply.

## Mas Rechisha (purchase tax) - reduced bracket

Israel's standard purchase tax (Mas Rechisha) starts at 0% for first-time buyers and 8% for investors. Olim get a **special middle bracket** for their first Israeli home:

| Price band | Standard first-home rate | **Olim rate** |
|---|---|---|
| Up to ₪1,978,745 | 0% | **0.5%** |
| ₪1,978,746 - ₪2,347,040 | 3.5% | **5%** |
| Above ₪2,347,040 | 5% | **5%** |

This **looks** worse than the standard first-home brackets, but here's why it matters: olim are usually treated as "investors" because they own (or owned) property abroad. Without the olim bracket, you'd pay **8% from the first shekel** - over ₪240,000 on a ₪3M apartment. With the olim bracket, the same ₪3M apartment costs you ~₪60,000.

**Eligibility window: 7 years from your aliyah date** (the date on your teudat oleh / teudat zehut). You can claim once for one apartment in that window. After 7 years, you're treated normally - first home gets the first-home brackets if you don't own abroad anymore, or investor rates if you do.

See our [Mas Rechisha guide](/guides/mas-rechisha-purchase-tax) for the calculation details.

## Misrad HaKlita (Aliyah Ministry) mortgage

The Aliyah Ministry offers a low-interest supplemental mortgage to olim buying their first Israeli home. As of 2026:

- **Amount**: ~₪139,000 (the exact figure adjusts annually; varies by family size, location, and whether you're a young couple, single parent, or otherwise)
- **Interest**: ~4% fixed (well below bank market rate)
- **Term**: 20-25 years
- **Eligibility**: First Israeli home, purchased within 15 years of aliyah

This is a **second-position loan** - it sits on top of your primary bank mortgage. The bank's mortgage takes first claim on the property; the Misrad HaKlita loan is junior. Banks accept this arrangement routinely.

**How to apply**: Visit a Misrad HaKlita office (every major city has one) with your teudat oleh, teudat zehut, and proof of property purchase (signed contract). The approval takes 4-8 weeks.

## Arnona discount

In the first 24 months after aliyah, olim qualify for a steep municipal property tax discount:

- **Months 1-12**: 90% off arnona
- **Months 13-24**: 80% off

The discount applies to the **first Israeli apartment you occupy** (rented or owned). After month 24, you pay the standard rate.

**How to apply**: Bring your teudat oleh + lease/purchase contract to the arnona office of your city. They typically backdate the discount to your move-in date if you apply within the first year.

See our [Arnona guide](/guides/arnona-property-tax) for context.

## VAT exemption on new construction (recently expanded)

The Israeli government from time to time has offered **17% VAT exemption** on certain new-construction apartments purchased by first-time buyers and olim. As of 2026 this is a targeted program tied to specific developments in periphery cities (Be'er Sheva, Eilat, Karmiel, etc.).

When active, it's worth massive money - VAT on a ₪2M apartment is ~₪340,000. But:

- The eligible developments are usually in less-desirable areas
- Resale restrictions apply (typically you can't sell for 5 years without losing the benefit)
- The program has been started, paused, and restarted multiple times

**Check status**: Misrad HaShikun (Housing Ministry) website at the time of purchase.

## Mas Shevach (capital gains tax) - primary residence exemption

When you sell an apartment, Israel charges 25% capital gains tax (Mas Shevach) on the appreciation - *unless* it's your primary residence, in which case there's a **full exemption** (one sale every 18 months).

For olim, the primary-residence clock starts on your aliyah date, not on when you bought the apartment. So if you bought a Tel Aviv apartment 2 years before aliyah and lived in it for 3 years post-aliyah, you can sell with the full exemption - non-olim need 4 years of residence.

## Banking - special olim accounts

Most Israeli banks offer **special olim accounts** in the first 12-24 months after aliyah:

- Lower minimum balance
- Waived monthly account fees
- Easier mortgage pre-approval (banks accept foreign-income documentation that they'd normally reject)
- **Foreign-currency accounts** alongside shekel accounts (useful while you still receive income from abroad)

Worth opening at **more than one bank** before applying for a mortgage - competition between banks for olim mortgages is healthy and you can extract better rates.

## Practical timing tips

### Year 1 (most benefits active)

- Apply for the **90% arnona discount** in your first city of residence
- Open an olim bank account at 2-3 banks
- Get a **pre-approval** for a mortgage even if you're not buying yet (lets you tour with confidence)

### Years 2-5 (some benefits remain, others phase out)

- Year 2: arnona discount drops to 80%, then ends
- Mas Rechisha olim bracket still available
- Misrad HaKlita mortgage still available (15-year window)

### Year 7 (the cliff)

- Mas Rechisha olim bracket **ends**. If you've owned property abroad continuously, you'll be classified as an investor on your next Israeli purchase.
- Strongly consider **buying your Israeli home before year 7** if you intend to own here. The tax savings alone often exceed ₪200,000 on a typical purchase.

## Common mistakes olim make

### 1. Buying property abroad first and Israel second

If you own any property abroad on the day you sign the Israeli purchase contract, you're not eligible for the olim first-home bracket. The workaround: **sell your foreign property first**, then buy in Israel. Or buy in Israel first, then sell abroad later.

### 2. Not applying for arnona discount

Cities don't automatically know you're an oleh. The 90% discount in year 1 alone is worth ~₪6,000-₪12,000 for most apartments. **Always apply within the first month** of moving in.

### 3. Missing the 7-year Mas Rechisha window

A surprising number of olim wait too long to buy. By year 8, you've lost the special bracket and the tax bill on the same apartment is 4-5× larger. Don't wait if you intend to buy.

### 4. Not consulting a **tax-specialized** lawyer

A generalist real estate lawyer may miss olim-specific exemptions. The lawyer fee is the same; the tax savings can be 50× the fee.
